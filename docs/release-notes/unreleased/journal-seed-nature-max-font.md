## Journal catalog: Nature now checks the 7 pt maximum text size and lists the panel-label style

Nature's author guidance says text should be 5 pt at minimum and 7 pt at maximum, and panel labels should be 8 pt bold upright letters. The built-in Nature entry only recorded the 5 pt minimum, so figures with text larger than 7 pt were not flagged against Nature. It now records the maximum, and shows the panel-label requirement as a reference row (not checked automatically).
