## Reopening a project no longer drops what you just did when the last layout can't be opened

If Tavotto couldn't bring back your last layout when you reopened a project, dragging a figure onto the canvas, pasting, or double-clicking a figure in the library used to do nothing except show "Your last layout isn't open yet." Now the action is kept and carried out. When the last layout is gone and there is nothing else to continue from, Tavotto quietly starts a new layout and puts the figure in it. When the layout is only temporarily unreadable, it tries once more on its own. When there are other layouts you could continue from, the empty canvas shows the choice right in the middle, and whatever you pick, your figure lands there. Figures handed over from an AI editor follow the same rules. Your older layout files are never changed, and Tavotto still never opens an older layout in place of the missing one without asking.
