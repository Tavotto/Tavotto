## AI cost estimates cover more models, and show the right currency

The estimated cost shown for the built-in AI now covers all current Anthropic models and the pay-as-you-go models from Zhipu and MiniMax, in addition to OpenAI's GPT-6 and 5.6 families. Providers that bill in Chinese yuan are shown with ¥ instead of $, and amounts in different currencies are no longer added together. The models suggested for an OpenAI API key in "Connect AI" are now `gpt-6.2-sol` and `gpt-6-luna`. Providers with time-of-day pricing, subscription plans or no published per-token price still show no estimate.
