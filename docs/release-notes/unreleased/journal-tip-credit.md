## Journal tips can carry your name (optional)

When you send a journal tip ("Looks wrong" on a source card, or "Not listed?" in the picker), the feedback window gains an unticked *Credit me in the catalog (visible to all users)* box. Tick it and type a name, and if the tip is accepted and changes a catalog entry, that entry's source card shows *Tip from <name>* to every user. Leave it unticked and nothing changes: no name is sent. Editor-wording tips and agent-submitted tips never carry a name. To have a name removed, reply to your letter. The credit only appears once the catalog release that includes it is published; with `TAVOTTO_NO_JOURNAL_SYNC=1` nothing is requested.
