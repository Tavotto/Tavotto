## Pick a journal and check your figure against its requirements

Settings › Specifications has a new "Journal" row: choose one and the problem panel checks against that journal's artwork rules, and the page width follows one of its columns. Each requirement has a "Source" button that shows the sentence it came from, highlighted on the journal's own page; anything the journal doesn't state is shown as not specified instead of being filled in.

Beyond the journals that ship with the app, the full catalog is downloaded one journal at a time when you pick it (up to 20 journals a day per computer, shared with connected AI agents). Set `TAVOTTO_NO_JOURNAL_SYNC=1` to turn downloading off.
