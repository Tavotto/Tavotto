## Ask the assistant what's wrong with a figure, then turn its answer into a to-do list

Ask "what's wrong with this figure?" and the assistant now just answers, as a numbered list, without touching anything. Click "Turn into to-dos" under the answer and it becomes a short to-do card: each line says what would change and why, everything is ticked to start with, and the preview follows your ticks. Vague requests like "go with your suggestions" or "make it look better" give you that card directly. You can untick lines, add another with "Add another…", and one click on "Make N changes" applies the in-figure changes as a single step, then makes the script changes one at a time (script backed up first, undoable). Changes that would alter the data being plotted still ask you first. If the assistant's script agent replies without changing anything, you now see what it said.

The assistant now reads what Tavotto knows about the figure (where the legend actually sits, the data range, how many points each line has) and the publication standard you picked, so its advice matches your journal: it no longer suggests ranges that would hide data, resizes only in ways the standard allows, and keeps continuous curves free of per-point markers.
