## Annotations inside a figure move and scale with it

In a multi-figure layout, text labels, arrows and shapes that sit entirely inside a figure now move with it when you drag the figure or nudge it with the arrow keys, and scale with it when you drag a corner. Annotations that lie outside the figure or cross its edge stay where they are; locked or hidden annotations never move, and a grouped annotation follows only when its whole group is inside. The "With annotations" button in the floating bar is gone, since this now happens automatically. Previously annotations never followed a moved figure, and the scaling option also pulled in annotations that only touched the figure's edge.

## Packages settings: the install row no longer sits inside an extra box

In Settings › Packages, the package name field and the "Search PyPI" and "Install" buttons now sit directly under the "User-installed" heading instead of inside a grey card.
