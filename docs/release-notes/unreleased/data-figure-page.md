## Data to figure: draw a figure straight from a data file (experimental, off by default)

Behind the experimental `ai.taf.enabled` switch, which is off by default, the home screen offers a second way in for people who have data but no figure yet: pick a folder of data and Tavotto lists the tables it found in a "Data" section of the left rail. Click a data file and a temporary tab opens next to your figures: Tavotto proposes a few ways to draw it, shows their names right away and fills in the thumbnails one by one from your real data. The side panel shows which columns were used (X axis, Y axis, grouping, and what happens to cells that are not numbers) so you can change them, and the data sits in a strip underneath. Click one candidate and the tab turns into the real figure, with a new script written next to your data in `tavotto_figures/`. Your data files are never changed, and the temporary tab is not part of your project. Nothing changes while the switch is off.
