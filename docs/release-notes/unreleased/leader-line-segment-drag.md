## Leader lines can be reshaped by dragging a segment

Lines drawn in axes or figure coordinates (such as a dashed leader line from a map point to its label) used to move only as a whole, which pulled the end off its marker. Now pressing on a segment drags just that segment: its two ends move together and the neighbouring segments stretch, so the line stays connected. Selecting the line shows a small square at each bend; drag one to move only that point. Hold Shift to keep the move horizontal or vertical. Moving the whole line still works with the arrow keys or by selecting it together with its label. Data curves still cannot be moved, and very long lines (over 64 points) still move only as a whole.
