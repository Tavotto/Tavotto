## Copy text from inside a figure as a text annotation

While editing a figure's contents, select a piece of text in it (title, axis label, tick number, legend entry or any text you added with the script), then press Cmd/Ctrl+C or right-click and choose *Copy as annotation*, and paste with Cmd/Ctrl+V or *Paste as annotation*. You get a text annotation on the page that sits on top of the original, offset 4 mm to the lower right like a duplicate, with the same wording (formulas included), size, colour, bold or italic, alignment, rotation and font: a y-axis label comes out rotated, and it stays rotated the right way when the figure itself is rotated or flipped. The copy is placed from the figure's own text layout, so before the offset it lies right on the original. Curves, filled areas and other things that are not text show "... can't be copied yet" in the top bar instead.
