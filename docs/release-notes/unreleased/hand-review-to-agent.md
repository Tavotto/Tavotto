## Hand review comments to the Agent, then see the difference before applying

When a figure has open comments from an external review, the Review tab shows a floating **Hand to Agent** button. The assistant reads the comments and sends back a card with the revised figure: open it to compare before and after (slide, side by side, or toggle), untick any change you don't want, and apply it in one step — ⌘Z undoes it. Nothing changes on your figure until you apply. Applying a plan marks the matching comments as handled on the review service too (undoing restores them); each one gets a short reply draft you can edit and send, and the reply is only sent when you press Send, and you choose when to update the review link. The comment text and the figure's list of editable elements go to the AI connection you picked; reviewer names are not sent. Single figures only for now.
