## True-scale in-page manuscript preview in the export dialog

The export dialog preview pane now includes a **Figure | In-page** switcher (`原图预览 | 版面预览`) that places your figure at true 1:1 millimeter scale onto an academic A4 manuscript sheet matching the active publication profile’s column geometry (single-column float, two-column spanning float, or print-margin overflow indicator), while preserving wheel zoom and pan inspection.
