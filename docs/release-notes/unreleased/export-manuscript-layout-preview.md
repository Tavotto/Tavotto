## True-scale in-page manuscript preview in the export dialog

The export dialog preview pane now includes a centered **Figure | In-page** switcher (`原图预览 | 版面预览`) that places your figure at true 1:1 millimeter scale onto a 96-DPI vector-scaled A4 journal manuscript specimen (with realistic two-column serif prose, math equations, and `Fig. 1` caption), supports cycling across common single-column and two-column journal float positions (`换位置` / `Position`), and clearly notes that surrounding article text is sample copy for scale preview only while export saves just the figure itself.
