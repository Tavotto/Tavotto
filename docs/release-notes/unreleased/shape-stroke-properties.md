## More stroke options for shapes and arrows

Rectangles, ellipses, triangles, diamonds and polygons can now have no outline: turn the stroke off in the Appearance panel and the fill covers the whole shape on the canvas and in every export format. Shapes and arrows also gained stroke opacity, three more dash styles (long dash, dash-dot, long dash-dot), round-dot, diamond and stealth arrowheads with small, medium and large sizes, and line cap and corner style options. The less common ones are under "More" in the Appearance panel.

Existing projects look and export exactly as before; the new settings only appear once you change them. Stroke opacity below 100% cannot be written to EPS (the same limit as fill opacity), so that format reports it and the other formats export normally.
