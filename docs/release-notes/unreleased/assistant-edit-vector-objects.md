## The figure assistant can now change the arrows, text and shapes you select on the canvas

Select a few arrows, labels, shapes or paths, tell the assistant what you want ("make these arrows 0.75 pt", "delete the box", "left-align the labels"), and it shows a change plan card first: the plain-words list of every change, plus a before-and-after of the whole canvas. Nothing is written until you press Apply, Apply is one step you can undo with Cmd/Ctrl+Z, and if you edited one of those objects in the meantime the card tells you the figure changed instead of overwriting your work. Only the selected canvas objects and the outlines of your figures are sent to the AI model you connected; your scripts, data and file names are not. Lines and bars drawn by your script are still changed through the figure itself.
