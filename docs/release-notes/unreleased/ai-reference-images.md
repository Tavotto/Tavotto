## Attach reference images in the AI assistant

The assistant's input box now takes pictures: click the image button, paste a screenshot, or drop image files in (up to 4). The AI you connected looks at them before editing, so you can say "match the colors of this figure". Large or unusual images are shrunk or converted automatically, and if the current model can't see images Tavotto says so and offers one that can. Images stay on your computer and go only to your own AI; the task history only remembers how many you attached.
