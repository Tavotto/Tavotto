## The exported `_edited.py` now carries your error bar, stem, bar and scatter edits

Colour, width, cap size, transparency and visibility of error bars; colour, line style, markers and visibility of stem plots; the fill, outline, hatch and visibility of a single bar, and a bar series' hatch width, hatch colour, bar width and name; scatter size, marker, line style, hatch and colour map; and curve and series names are now written into the `_edited.py` file you get by ticking **PY** on export. Before, these edits were left out and the script drew those series as it originally did.
