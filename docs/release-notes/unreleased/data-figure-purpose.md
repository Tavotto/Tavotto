## From data to figure: tell Tavotto what you want to see (experimental)

In the experimental "from data to figure" flow, the same table can now be looked at for a purpose you pick — compare groups, see a trend, see a relationship, or see a distribution — and the candidate figures are re-ordered to match your pick. Nothing new is drawn and nothing the rules had ruled out comes back; only the order changes. Previewing a candidate that was not recommended can also say, in one line, why it is a weaker fit for that purpose. Without a pick, the candidates and their order are exactly as before.
