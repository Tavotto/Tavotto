## Sharing for review no longer pushes the dialog around while it uploads

When you click "Generate link" or "Update this link" in Share for review, the dialog keeps its exact size. Progress is a thin line that runs around the edge of the dialog and closes the loop when the link is ready, then the link slides into place. If the upload fails, the reason replaces the small line at the bottom with a "Retry" next to it, instead of a red box appearing below the dialog.
