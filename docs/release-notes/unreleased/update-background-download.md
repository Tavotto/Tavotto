## Desktop: new versions download in the background and install when you quit

Updates used to wait for you to click through a "new version" dialog and then sit through a download of up to a couple of hundred MB. Now, when Tavotto finds a new version at startup it quietly downloads and verifies it in the background, and a small "Update ready" pill appears in the top bar. Click it to restart into the new version right away, or ignore it: the update installs when you quit, so the next launch is already the new version. Your running session is never swapped out from under you.

If you would rather decide yourself, turn off **Settings > Updates > Download updates in the background**; Tavotto then asks first and downloads only after you accept, as before. A failed background download stays silent, and you can still download manually from Settings. Download progress also no longer makes the interface sluggish on slow connections.
