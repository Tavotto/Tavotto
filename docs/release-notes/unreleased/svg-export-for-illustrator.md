## SVG exports open in Illustrator with your groups and names

Exported SVGs now keep the groups you made and the names you gave objects, so in Illustrator they show up as named groups and layers you can ungroup and edit piece by piece instead of a flat pile of unnamed shapes. A clipping group is written once instead of being copied onto every object, and text asks for a font Illustrator actually has (Times New Roman, Arial, Songti SC and so on) before the bundled ones, so it no longer gets swapped for an unrelated font. PDF and PNG exports are unchanged.
