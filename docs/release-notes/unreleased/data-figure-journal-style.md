## Figures drawn from data follow the journal you picked

In the experimental "draw from data" page (still off by default), a new Journal row at the top of the right-hand panel lets you pick a journal without opening Settings. Candidates and the created figure are then drawn at that journal's column widths, with text kept inside the journal's font-size range: it starts from the largest allowed size and steps a whole kind of text (tick labels, axis titles, or legend) down together only when labels would collide. The panel shows the widths and font range actually used. A legend now stays inside the plot when it does not cover any data and moves above the plot when it would. The journal picker (here and in Settings) shows your choice immediately instead of waiting for the journal's requirements to download.
