## Gradients, live corners and the shape builder

Fills and strokes of shapes and paths can be linear or radial gradients. Edit the colour stops on a strip in the inspector, or drag the gradient handles directly on the canvas. Drag the dot inside a corner of a rectangle or a sharp path corner to round it. The shape builder (Shift-M) splits overlapping shapes into regions you drag across to merge or Option-click to remove. The colour picker adds the colours used in the figures on the canvas and a colour-blind-safe palette.
