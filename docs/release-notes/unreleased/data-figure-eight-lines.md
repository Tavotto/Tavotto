## Data-to-figure can draw up to eight chosen columns as lines, each in its own colour

- When you pick several measured columns for the vertical axis of a trend figure, up to eight can now be drawn together, one line each (the limit was three). Tavotto still picks at most three on its own; the higher limit only applies to columns you choose.
- The colour set used for groups and lines gains an eighth colour, a neutral grey. Before, a figure with eight groups drew the eighth group in the same colour as the first. The first seven colours are unchanged.
