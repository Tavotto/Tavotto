## Use your ChatGPT plan directly: “Continue with ChatGPT”

You can now connect your ChatGPT subscription in Settings › AI (OpenAI › subscription sign-in) with **Continue with ChatGPT**: sign in once in your browser and Tavotto uses your plan — no Codex CLI to install. It works everywhere an API key does (editing figures, AI fix, data-to-figure), including reasoning effort for models that support it. The signed-in row shows your account email and has **Sign out**, which also revokes Tavotto’s access.

If you previously connected ChatGPT through the Codex login, sign in once with **Continue with ChatGPT**; the Codex login option is no longer listed. Usage counts against your ChatGPT plan (manage it in ChatGPT settings). To turn this off, set `TAVOTTO_EXPERIMENTAL_SIWC=0`.
