## Copy and paste figure text without leaving figure editing

When you edit inside a figure, Cmd/Ctrl+C then Cmd/Ctrl+V on a title, axis label, or text box now makes a copy of that text inside the same figure, slightly down and to the right, and selects it. You stay in figure editing, so you can restyle or move the copy right away, and one undo removes it. Cmd/Ctrl+D does the same thing in place; before, it duplicated the whole figure. The right-click menu on a figure element now offers Copy, Paste, Duplicate, and Paste as annotation; the old Copy as annotation item is gone because copying already carries the annotation version too. Pasting somewhere else (the canvas, another figure, another tab) still gives you a text annotation, and tick numbers, legend text, 3D axes, and colorbars still copy as annotations only. A copy can be deleted from its right-click menu, and it carries into exported figures and the edited .py, but "Apply these edits to other figures from the same script" skips copies.
