## One-step "fix to journal" for AI editors

Tell your AI editor which journal you are submitting to and it can now bring the open figure into line in one go: `tavotto_fix_to_journal` picks the column width, resizes the page, raises and swaps fonts, snaps line widths and tidies ticks, legend frames and borders, checking every step against a real render and rolling everything back if the fix would introduce a new problem. If the figure could be single or double column it asks you first instead of guessing. It shows before and after previews, and `dry_run` lets you see what would change without touching anything. Your script is never edited, and nothing is exported unless you ask for it. The preflight report now also tells the AI, for each problem, whether Tavotto can fix it or the script has to change.
