## Data to figure: choose which AI analyses your data, or turn it off

In the experimental, off-by-default data-to-figure page, a small button at the bottom-left of the Data drawer now lets you pick which connected AI is used to work out what each column is, or choose "Don't analyze with AI". When it is off, the page sends nothing to any model. If the AI you picked becomes unavailable, Tavotto does not switch to another one on its own.
