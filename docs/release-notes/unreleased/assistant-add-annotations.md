## The assistant can add frames, panel letters and brackets on the canvas

Ask the figure assistant to frame a panel, put (a) (b) labels on the panels, or draw a significance bracket between two groups, and it now proposes the new objects in the same change plan card as its edits: you see them in the list and the before-and-after, can uncheck any step, and Apply adds them in one step you can undo. Stars and p-values are only added when they come from your own words; otherwise the card asks you first. It can also point an arrow at a specific peak or data point and place labels next to curves: it measures your figure as it currently looks (including edits you haven't saved), and if a figure can't be measured it falls back to the panel's outline and says so on the card.
