## The assistant can point at a peak or a data point

Asked to "put an arrow on the peak" or "label the higher curve", the figure assistant now measures your figure as it looks right now, including edits you haven't saved and the latest data file, and places the arrow tip or label on the exact point. If a figure can't be measured (a frozen snapshot, a figure without a script, or one that takes too long), the annotation is placed by the panel's outline instead and the card says so. Measuring uses a separate one-time run, so the figure on your canvas keeps rendering normally.
