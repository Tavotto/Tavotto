## Share a figure or layout for review with a link

Press *Share for review* next to *Export* to turn the figure or layout you're looking at into a link. Whoever opens it can comment right on the picture in a browser (click an element, drag a box, or leave a general note) without installing Tavotto or signing in; their comments show up in a new *Review* tab in the right panel, where you can reply and mark them done. After you change the figure, *Update this link* adds a new version to the same link. Only the rendered picture and the positions of its elements are uploaded, never your scripts, data or file paths, and nothing is sent until you press *Create link* or *Update this link*. Each device can create one new link per day; updating an existing link isn't limited. Links expire 30 days after the last update, and *Stop sharing* deletes them right away. Set `TAVOTTO_NO_REVIEW_SHARE=1` to remove the feature and all of its network requests.
