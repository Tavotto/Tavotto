## Attach files in the AI assistant

The assistant's input box now takes files: click the paperclip, paste a screenshot, or drop files in (up to 6, any format). Pictures are shown to the AI you connected, so you can say "match the colors of this figure"; data files such as CSV or XLSX are handed over for it to read. Large or unusual images are shrunk or converted automatically, and if the current model can't see images Tavotto says so and offers one that can. Attached files stay on your computer and go only to your own AI; the task history only remembers how many you attached.
