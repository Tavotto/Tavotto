## Problems now flags a legend entry that doesn't look like its curve

When a legend entry's line color, line style, line width, marker shape or marker color differs from the curve (or bar) it labels — for example after the curve was recolored but a hand-styled legend handle wasn't — the **Problems** panel lists it as a warning, and clicking it selects that legend entry. The figure assistant also sees the mismatch: asked what is wrong, to make the figure nicer, or to follow the publication standard, it points it out and offers to restyle the legend entry to match.
