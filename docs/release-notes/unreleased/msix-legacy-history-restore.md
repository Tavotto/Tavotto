## Bring back AI tasks from before you moved to the desktop version

If you used Tavotto on Windows from inside another app (such as Codex) and some of your earlier AI tasks did not come over, the task history now shows one line at the bottom saying how many are missing, with a "Bring back" button. It asks once before doing anything. Tasks you deleted since then come back too, because they cannot be told apart from ones that were never copied. Nothing changes until you confirm, and the line disappears once they are back.
