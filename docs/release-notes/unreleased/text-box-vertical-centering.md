## Text and formulas sit centred in text boxes, and tall formulas no longer spill out

Chinese, Latin text and inline formulas in canvas text annotations now sit vertically centred in their line, and the canvas preview matches the exported file (previously the export placed text about 0.1 em higher than the preview, #1669). A tall formula — a fraction or a sum with limits — now makes its own line taller instead of sticking out of the box above and below. Vertical text follows the same rule: sideways Latin text and formulas are centred in their column, and a tall formula widens its own column. Copying a figure's text as an annotation keeps the first baseline in place when the first line holds a tall formula. Existing horizontal annotations export about 0.1 em lower than before, and sideways text in vertical annotations about 0.1 em further left; boxes containing tall formulas grow to fit them. (#1972)
