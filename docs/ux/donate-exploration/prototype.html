探索已经足够艰难，表达不该再添阻碍。

Tavotto is an open-source editor for scientific figures. It lets you finish the figure your script drew without rewriting the script. This page is about why we build it, and how you can help.

Tavotto 是一个开源的科研图形编辑器。它让你直接完成脚本画出的那张图，而不必一次次重写脚本。这一页讲我们为什么做它，以及你可以怎样帮助它继续下去。

Fig. 1 Dose–response, illustrative data drawn for this page. Points, mean ± s.d. of three replicates; line, four-parameter Hill fit. The legend is selected: in Tavotto, everything around the data can be shaped.

图 1 剂量–反应关系，为本页绘制的示意数据。点为三次重复的均值 ± 标准差；线为四参数 Hill 拟合。图例处于选中状态：在 Tavotto 里，数据周围的一切都可以被塑造。

IIThe problem问题II / VI

The last millimetre shouldn’t take another night.

最后一毫米，
不该再耗掉一个夜晚。

Every published figure carries hours that nobody sees.

A legend that covers the last data point. Tick labels a point too small for the journal. Panel (b) sitting a millimetre lower than panel (a). Each fix means changing a line, running the script again, opening the file and looking. Then doing it again.

The science was finished long before the figure was. The distance between a result and a figure that shows it well is where Tavotto works.

每一张发表出来的图，背后都有无人看见的几个小时。

图例压住了最后一个数据点；刻度标签比期刊要求小了一号；(b) 面板比 (a) 低了一毫米。每一处修改，都意味着改一行代码、重新运行脚本、打开文件、再看一眼。然后，再来一遍。

科学结论早已完成，图却还没有。结果与一张恰当呈现它的图之间的这段距离，正是 Tavotto 工作的地方。

IIIThe principle原则III / VI

Keep the source. Shape the figure.

保留脚本，雕琢图形。

Tavotto opens the figure your script has already drawn, and lets you shape it directly.

Legends, axes, type, and the arrangement of panels on a page measured in millimetres. Each change is kept beside the figure rather than written into your code, and replayed whenever the script runs again.

Tavotto 打开脚本已经画好的图，让你直接塑造它。

图例、坐标轴、文字，以及面板在以毫米度量的页面上的排布。每一处修改都保存在图的旁边，而不是写进你的代码；脚本再次运行时，这些修改会被重新应用。

As the script drew it.

脚本画出的样子。

A figure the way a script usually leaves it: correct, complete, and not yet ready. The legend covers the last points of panel (b). The axis labels are small. The panels don’t line up.

这是脚本通常留下的一张图：正确、完整，但还没有准备好。图例压住了 (b) 面板最后几个点，坐标轴标签太小，两个面板也没有对齐。

02 / 05

Move the legend.

移动图例。

Pick it up and put it where it belongs. Its position is kept, in millimetres, beside the figure. Nothing in the script has changed.

把它拿起来，放到该在的位置。位置以毫米记录在图的旁边。脚本一个字都没有改。

03 / 05

Set the type.

调整字号。

Axis labels from 7 to 9 pt, as the journal asks. The same words your script wrote, at the size the page needs.

坐标轴标签从 7 pt 调到期刊要求的 9 pt。还是脚本写下的那些字，只是换成了版面需要的大小。

04 / 05

Align the panels.

对齐面板。

Panel (b) rises to meet panel (a), and both receive their labels. The page is measured in millimetres, so what aligns here aligns in print.

(b) 面板上移，与 (a) 对齐，两者都加上面板标号。页面以毫米度量——在这里对齐的，印出来也对齐。

05 / 05

The data stays where it was.

数据留在原处。

Curves, points and error bars come from your script, and only from your script. Tavotto makes a figure easier to shape — never its data easier to change.

曲线、数据点与误差棒来自你的脚本，也只来自你的脚本。Tavotto 让图更容易被塑造，但绝不让数据更容易被改动。

IVThe invisible work看不见的工作IV / VI

Good tools take work you may never see.

好的工具，
背后有许多你看不见的工作。

A figure editor is mostly made of things that should never be noticed.

They are noticed only when they fail: a font that turns into boxes, an export that quietly rasterises the text, a change that is lost on the next run. Here is some of that work, as it stands in October 2026.

一个图形编辑器，大部分由本不该被注意到的东西构成。

只有出错时它们才会被注意到：字体变成方框，导出时文字被悄悄栅格化，一处修改在下一次运行时丢失。下面是其中一部分工作，截至 2026 年 10 月。

A good day, recorded. Eight things happen; nothing appears to.记录下来的顺利的一天：发生了八件事，看起来却什么都没发生。01–08 below见下方 01–08
01

Your Python, as it is.

就用你现有的 Python。

Python 3.10 to 3.14 and matplotlib 3.8 to 3.11 — in the runtime Tavotto carries, or in your project’s own environment, which is in beta.

Python 3.10 至 3.14，matplotlib 3.8 至 3.11——可以用 Tavotto 自带的运行环境，也可以用你项目自己的环境（Beta）。

02

Tested where you work.

在你工作的地方测试。

macOS on Apple silicon and Intel, and Windows x64: each with an installer, CI checks and a smoke test of the real build. Linux runs from PyPI in a browser tab, in beta.

macOS（Apple 芯片与 Intel）与 Windows x64：都有安装包、CI 检查，并对真实构建产物做冒烟测试。Linux 通过 PyPI 在浏览器标签页中运行，目前为 Beta。

03

Nothing written until it is proven.

未经证明，一字不写。

Before a change is written back into a figure file, the script runs again from scratch and the result is compared with what you saw — geometry and pixels. If anything differs, the file is left exactly as it was.

在把修改写回图形文件之前，脚本会从头再运行一次，结果与你所见逐一比对——几何与像素。只要有任何不同，原文件就保持原样，一字不动。

04

Text that stays text.

文字始终是文字。

PDF export embeds each vector panel as it was drawn, so text remains real, selectable, searchable text. PNG and TIFF are rasterised from that same PDF.

PDF 导出按原样嵌入每个矢量面板，文字始终是真实的、可选中、可搜索的文字。PNG 与 TIFF 从同一份 PDF 栅格化而来。

05

Checked before it leaves.

离开之前，先做检查。

Before export, the figure is checked against a versioned publication profile: one set of rules, read by both the engine and the interface.

导出前，图会依据一份带版本的出版规范接受检查：同一套规则，引擎与界面共读。

06

Failure, said plainly.

失败就说失败。

When a refresh fails, the status says so. Approximate previews are marked as approximate. Properties Tavotto cannot edit are listed, not hidden.

刷新失败时，状态如实说明。近似预览会标明是近似。Tavotto 无法编辑的属性会被列出来，而不是藏起来。

07

Your figures stay on your machine.

你的图留在你的电脑上。

Everything runs locally. Telemetry is off until you say yes, and your scripts, paths and the text inside your figures are structurally unable to leave.

一切都在本机运行。在你同意之前，遥测保持关闭；你的脚本、路径与图中的文字，在结构上就无法被发送出去。

08

Decisions, written down.

每个决定，都有记录。

More than a hundred architecture decision records, kept in public beside the code — including the ones that say what Tavotto will not do.

一百多份架构决策记录，与代码一起公开存放——也包括那些写明 Tavotto 不做什么的决定。

Much of this work is invisible when everything goes right. All of it matters.

一切顺利时，这些工作大多不为人见。但每一项都很重要。

VThe invitation邀请V / VI

Help us keep building.

帮助我们继续做下去。

Tavotto is free and open source, under the AGPL. It will stay that way.

What it needs to keep going is time: time to follow Python and matplotlib as they change, to test on the machines researchers actually use, and to do the slow, careful work described above.

If Tavotto has given you back an evening — or if you believe researchers deserve tools made with this much care — you can help pay for that time.

Tavotto 是免费的开源软件，采用 AGPL 许可证，并将一直如此。

让它继续走下去，需要的是时间：跟上 Python 与 matplotlib 的变化，在科研人员真正使用的电脑上测试，以及完成上面那些缓慢而细致的工作。

如果 Tavotto 曾为你省下一个夜晚——或者你相信，科研人员值得拥有被如此认真对待的工具——你可以帮我们支付这些时间。
